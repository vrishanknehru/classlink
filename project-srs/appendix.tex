\hypertarget{evaluation-plan-and-data-schema}{%
\section{Evaluation Plan and Data Schema}\label{evaluation-plan-and-data-schema}}

\textbf{Primary metric.} Detection success rate: the fraction of labelled position scan attempts (front, middle, back, door, corridor, adjacent room) that accept or reject correctly under the configured policy.

\textbf{Secondary metrics.} Discovery latency, attendance completion latency, false accept and false reject rates at out of room and in room positions, API p50 and p95 latency, and the rejection rate for duplicate claims and expired tokens.

\textbf{Method.} One teacher phone and at least two student phones of different models, in a real classroom. Record raw rows before computing any summary.

\textbf{Raw CSV schema} (\texttt{docs/evaluation/*.csv}):
